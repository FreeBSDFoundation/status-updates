## **Reporting Period:** 2026-11-04 - 2026-11-10

### Project: Improve OpenJDK on FreeBSD
#### **Overall Status:** On Track
#### **Summary**

Worked on the mainline BSD port to fix the accuracy of thread CPU time measurements, and worked on reducing the changes needed to support extended attributes in the filesystem on BSD. (Some more work is needed before this is completed.) Also helped submit a couple of patches coming from others into the ports and mainline BSD port repo respectively. Finally updated OpenJDK 11 to apply the upstream eMudhra CA certificate fix.

#### **Completed Tasks**

- Mainline BSD port:
  - [battleblow/jdk #85] Fix `os::thread_cpu_time` for current thread on BSD
  - [battleblow/jdk #86] Refactor (Bsd|Unix)UserDefinedFileAttributeView
  - Reviewed and merged [battleblow/jdk #84] Read the kevent ident at its full width in KQueue.getDescriptor (by pkubaj@)
  - [battleblow/jdk #87] Merge upstream changes + some fixes
- Backports:
