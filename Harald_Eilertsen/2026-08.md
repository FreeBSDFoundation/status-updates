## **Reporting Period:** 2026-08-12 - 2026-08-18

### Project: Improve OpenJDK on FreeBSD
#### **Overall Status:** On Track
#### **Summary**

A somewhat short week, and mainly worked on [Bug #297282], and trying out
various alternatives. Ended up with removing the `USE_LDCONFIG` lines from the
openjdk 8 and 11 ports.

Also looking at other relevant bugs and keeping the bsd-port updated.

shared objects, so that it's again possible to enable hidden symbols by default
for libjvm.so.
