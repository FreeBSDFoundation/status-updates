### Project: SBOM Solution for FreeBSD (STA WPE)

#### **Overall Status:** On Track

#### **Summary**

Software Bills of Materials are becoming an important part of software
supply-chain security. They help organizations understand what components are
included in the software they use and where vulnerabilities may exist.

This is especially important for FreeBSD because it is incorporated into many
downstream products, appliances, services, and other systems.

Developing reliable SBOM tooling upstream would give downstream users
consistent information they can use in their own security and compliance
processes. It also avoids requiring every organization to build its own
solution.

#### **Completed Tasks**

* Added support for namespaces in [D56474](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D56474).
  This should help avoid conflicts with any solution provided for ports (like
  e.g., [D60441](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D60441)), be more consistent with
  the names of FreeBSD packages (1:1 match is not really possible), and it
  helped find and correct gaps in the meta-data from
  [D60352](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D60352).

#### **Features Delivered**

### Project: CRA security and vulnerability management

#### **Summary**

The Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) is an EU regulation establishing cybersecurity
requirements in the EU, including vulnerability reporting and security updates.
The FreeBSD Foundation considers itself an Open Source Software Steward under
the CRA due to its sustained support for the FreeBSD Project, which is intended
for commercial activities. Consequently, it is subject to the specific, limited
obligations applicable to such stewards under Article 24.

The Open Regulatory Compliance Working Group (ORC WG) from the Eclipse
Foundation helps Open Source maintainers and users meet the compliance
requirements of the CRA.

The FreeBSD Foundation participates to the ORC WG in its capacity as interface
and facilitator between the users, maintainers, upstreams, and downstreams of
the FreeBSD Project. The objective is to achieve and support compliance with
the CRA for the FreeBSD community at large.
