### **Project:** Virtio Balloon Device for Bhyve

#### **Summary/Completed Tasks**

* Sent the updated balloon implementation for review as a six-revision stack: [D61067](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61067) (shared guest-memory discard interface), [D61068](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61068) (newer memory statistics tags), [D61069](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61069) (virtio-balloon device with target control and guest statistics), [D61070](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61070) (save/restore state across checkpoints), [D61071](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61071) (populated guest-memory query), and [D61072](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61072) (avoid populating guest memory during checkpoint/restore).
* [D61069](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61069) adds the traditional virtio-balloon device for amd64 and arm64, with synchronous inflation using the new `VM_DISCARD_PAGES` interface, up to 1024 PFNs per request, on-demand guest statistics, opt-in deflate-on-OOM, and `bhyvectl` commands for setting the target and reading statistics over the IPC socket.
* Tested on native amd64 and arm64 with Alpine 3.24.3 over MSI-X and legacy interrupts: 1 GiB guests with 256/512 MiB targets reduced host residency to 768/512 MiB with no swap writes, all 16 statistics fields refreshed, and deflation/reinflation passed.
* Checkpoint/restore now preserves balloon state and avoids faulting untouched or ballooned pages: a 2 GiB guest with 1.2 GiB ballooned stayed at roughly 748 MiB of host residency during a live checkpoint (down from 2048 MiB) and restored at 639 MiB, with the checkpoint image format unchanged.
* Also sent [D61003](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61003) to reject unsupported checkpoint requests before any RAM is saved or existing checkpoint files are touched.
