# 11 2026 Status Update for Roman Bogorodskiy

## **Reporting Period:** [2026-11-2] - [2026-11-8]

### Project: libvirt on FreeBSD
#### **Overall Status:** On Track
#### **Summary**

This week I was working on extending bhyve capabilities reporting and
configuration validation. Specifically, I have published a patch for
configuration options validation in
[D61163](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61163), which awaits reviews now.

I have also re-tested the suspend/resume issue documented in
[PR 290735](https://bugs.freebsd.org/bugzilla/show_bug.cgi?id=290735),
and it no longer appears to be reproducible on a recent -CURRENT.

I also noticed a regression in IPC handling and submitted a fix in
[D61266](https://reviews.freebsd.org/D61266), which awaits reviews now.

I have published a
[patch series](https://patchew.org/Libvirt/20261106184825.18515-1-bogorodskiy@gmail.com/)
for libvirt/bhyve disk-related improvements and fixes (honor readonly,
sector size, and nodelete settings, honor the function component of PCI
addresses, and accept block devices).

I was also working on fixing an issue of libvirt/bhyve handling VMs
destroyed by SIGKILL. I will publish that for review next week.

I have resurrected my old branch where I was working on adding suspend/resume
support to libvirt and am now continuing to work on that.
